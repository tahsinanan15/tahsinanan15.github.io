How I got here

I grew up in Dhaka, Bangladesh, drawing on walls. At two I drew a cockroach, because why was it there? At ten, an art school told me I wouldn't start at level one. They put me at level three, with the older kids. Eyes became my favorite thing to draw. I came to New York to study at Baruch.

People open up to me. Friends, coworkers and the students I've mentored come to me for advice, and I listen.
